CONSTITUTION OF INDIA - RAG TEST QUERIES
=======================================
Format:   query  |  expected article(s)  |  why the baseline might fail
"Expected" = the chunk(s) that SHOULD be in the top 3. Whether the bi-encoder
actually misses them must be checked by running the query - these are
candidates, not guaranteed failures.


1. KEYWORD TRAP / DISTRACTOR
   (high word overlap with the wrong passage; Union vs State look-alikes)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Can the Governor pardon a person sentenced to death?            | 72 (clause 3), 161 | 161 matches "Governor ... pardon" but the death-sentence nuance is in Art. 72
What is the minimum age to become President of India?           | 58                 | 66 (Vice-President) and 157 (Governor) use the identical phrase "age of thirty-five years"
What is the minimum age to become a member of the Lok Sabha?    | 84                 | 173 (State Legislature) has the same "twenty-five years of age" wording
Who can issue Ordinances when the legislature is not in session? | 123, 213          | Union and State versions are near-duplicates; one may crowd out the other
Which court can issue a writ of habeas corpus?                  | 32, 226            | 139 (conferring extra writ powers on SC) shares the same wording
What is the maximum gap allowed between two sessions of Parliament? | 85             | 174 is the same rule for State Legislatures
Can the President be a member of Parliament?                    | 59                 | 66 and 158 contain the same "shall not be a member of either House" wording


2. SYNONYMS / DIFFERENT TERMINOLOGY
   (user's words are not the document's words)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Does an accused person have the right to remain silent?        | 20 (clause 3)      | Text says "compelled to be a witness against himself", never "silent"
Can the government ban a peaceful protest?                     | 19(1)(b), 19(3)    | Text says "assemble peaceably and without arms" and "public order"
Is child labour allowed in India?                              | 24                 | Text says "no child below the age of fourteen years shall be employed ... in any factory or mine"
Is human trafficking illegal?                                  | 23                 | Text says "traffic in human beings and begar and other similar forms of forced labour"
Can the President be sued in court?                            | 361                | Text says "not be answerable to any court"
How can the President be removed from office?                  | 56, 61             | Text uses "impeachment" and "violation of the Constitution"
At what age can a citizen vote?                                | 326                | Text says "adult suffrage" and "not less than eighteen years of age"
What is the national language of India?                        | 343                | Constitution only names an "official language" (Hindi in Devanagari) - no "national language"
Is caste discrimination illegal?                               | 15, 17             | 17 uses "Untouchability", 15 uses "caste" among a list of grounds


3. EXCEPTIONS & CORNER CASES
   (general rule retrieved, the "except / provided that / nothing in" missed)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Can the State make special laws only for women?                | 15 (clause 3)      | 15(1) bans sex discrimination - baseline may answer "no"
Can the State reserve government jobs for backward classes?    | 16 (clause 4)      | 16(1)-(2) say equal opportunity / no discrimination
Can fundamental rights be suspended during an emergency?       | 359                | Rule allows suspension EXCEPT articles 20 and 21
Can the right to life be suspended during an emergency?        | 359, 21            | Same as above - the exception is the whole answer
Does Article 13 invalidate constitutional amendments that violate fundamental rights? | 13 (clause 4) | 13(2) voids such laws; 13(4) excludes amendments under 368
Can a non-MP be appointed as a Union Minister?                 | 75 (clause 5)      | Allowed, but only for six consecutive months
Can a non-MLA become a Chief Minister or State Minister?       | 164 (clause 4)     | Same six-month rule, State version
Can citizens be stopped from moving freely in Scheduled Tribe areas? | 19(5)        | Freedom of movement is in 19(1)(d); the restriction is in 19(5)
On what grounds can free speech be restricted?                 | 19 (clause 2)      | 19(1)(a) grants the right; restrictions are in a separate chunk


4. NEGATION & CONSTRAINTS
   ("not allowed", "cannot", "who is excluded", role limits)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Who is NOT protected by the arrest and detention safeguards?   | 22 (clause 3)      | 22(1)-(2) are the safeguards; exclusions (enemy aliens, preventive detention) are in 22(3)
Within how many hours must an arrested person be produced before a magistrate? | 22 (clause 2) | Answer is 24 hours; nearby clauses discuss preventive detention periods
What can the Rajya Sabha NOT do with a Money Bill?             | 109                | Can only recommend within 14 days; 110 (definition) and 107 also say "Money Bill"
Which titles is the State NOT allowed to confer?               | 18                 | Exceptions for military and academic distinctions
Can a non-citizen holding a public office accept a foreign title without permission? | 18 (clause 3) | Role-specific constraint inside Art. 18
Is religious instruction allowed in government-funded schools? | 28                 | Banned in wholly State-funded schools, allowed in some others


5. OTHER DOCUMENT-SPECIFIC FAILURES
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
-- Article-number lookups (embeddings are weak with numbers) --
What does Article 21 say?                                      | 21                 | Bi-encoder often fails on bare numbers; cross-encoder can only re-rank the top 10
What is Article 356 about?                                     | 356                |
What is Article 370?                                           | 370                | Heavily discussed politically; check for hallucination from LLM's own knowledge

-- Omitted / moved articles --
Is the right to property a fundamental right?                  | 300A (and 31 Omitted, 19(1)(f) omitted) | Answer lives in Part XII, far from the Fundamental Rights chunks
What does Article 31 say?                                      | 31 (Omitted)       | Tests whether the LLM admits it was omitted instead of inventing text

-- Cross-references ("the said clause", "article 16") --
Can peaceful assembly be restricted in the interest of public order? | 19(3)        | 19(3) only says "sub-clause (b) of the said clause" - the word "assembly" is in 19(1)
Explanation of "economically weaker sections" for reservations | 15 (Explanation), 16(6) | The Explanation is shared "for the purposes of this article and article 16"

-- Headings that are not in the article text --
What does the right to equality say?                           | 14                 | "Right to Equality" is a section heading you strip; Art. 14 text never uses that phrase
What are the fundamental duties of citizens?                   | 51A                | Text says "It shall be the duty of every citizen"
What are the Directive Principles of State Policy?             | 36, 37             | Part heading; individual articles rarely repeat the phrase


6. OUT-OF-CORPUS / HALLUCINATION CHECKS
   (answer is NOT in the article text - good LLM should say so)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Is the right to privacy a fundamental right?                   | none in text       | Comes from a Supreme Court judgment, not the text
When was the right to education added to the Constitution?     | 21A + FOOTNOTE     | Year/amendment is only in the footnotes you removed - motivates v2
Which amendment inserted Article 21A?                          | FOOTNOTE           | Same - v1 should fail, v2 with footnotes should pass
What is the basic structure doctrine?                          | none in text       | Judicial doctrine (Kesavananda Bharati), not in the text
What does the First Schedule list?                             | none in corpus     | You excluded the Schedules (articles only)
